🇳🇬 Nigeria Edition

Nigeria Availability

Every platform on this page accepts Nigerian workers. Here is exactly how you get paid, what to expect, and the one thing that will get your account banned.

💳

Payoneer is your key

Most international platforms pay Nigerians through Payoneer (USD), which you then withdraw to your local bank. Set it up and verify it in week 1.

🏦

Naira-direct options exist

GoJelly and OpinionPadi claim to pay straight to Nigerian bank accounts — verify them with a small job first; treat GoTranscript/OneForma (Payoneer) as your proven base.

🔄

Task availability fluctuates

Every platform has dry days. That is exactly why you register on ALL of them, not just one — when one is empty, another pays.

✨

Mindrift for AI tasks

Mindrift is open to workers in Africa and pays via Payoneer/PayPal — good filler work when your main platforms are quiet.

🇳🇬

Outlier is limited for Nigeria

Outlier restricts many Nigerians from joining or withdrawing. Appen CrowdGen, OneForma and TELUS (Payoneer) are safer bets.

📱

Usability testing is bonus only

UserTesting, Userfeel and TryMyUI have limited Nigeria availability. Apply if you like, but do not count on them — treat any test you get as a bonus.

🚨

Never use a VPN to access a platform

Using a VPN violates platform terms and gets your account permanently banned — with any unpaid earnings lost. Log in from your real Nigerian location, always. Legitimate platforms accept Nigeria; you do not need to hide where you are.
